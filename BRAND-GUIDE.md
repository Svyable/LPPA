# Princess Puppy Adventures — Brand Guide

## Core promise

A warm, imaginative coloring world where Leia is a recurring character, not an interchangeable puppy drawing.

## Leia

Leia should remain recognizable from page to page through consistent:
- breed appearance;
- facial structure and expression;
- markings;
- ears;
- body proportions;
- crown/cape/accessories;
- curious, kind, brave personality.

## Story vocabulary

Favor magic, curiosity, courage, kindness, friendship, discovery, playfulness, tiny surprises, and emotional warmth.

Avoid scary material, cynical humor, generic clip-art staging, overcomplicated lore, visual clutter, or marketing language inside the child-facing experience.

## Visual vocabulary

- handmade and intentional rather than synthetic;
- clear expressive forms;
- strong black outlines;
- generous white space;
- limited micro-detail;
- inviting for roughly ages 4–8;
- a repeatable visual identity that can support future books.

## Public-site rule

The marketing site may use approved character/cover art once it is cleared for public use. Draft assets remain labeled as candidates and should not silently become brand-defining artwork.
