## Art direction

Leia should remain the same beloved puppy throughout: consistent breed appearance, facial structure, markings, ears, proportions, personality, accessories, and visual vocabulary.

Artwork should be warm, handmade, playful, intentional, age-appropriate, and easy to color. Favor strong clean black outlines, comfortable white space, expressive forms, and limited tiny closed areas. Avoid gray shading unless deliberately required.
