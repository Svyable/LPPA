## Print-art requirements

- strong, clean black outlines;
- no accidental gray shading;
- comfortable white space;
- age-appropriate complexity for approximately ages 4–8;
- no excessive tiny closed coloring areas;
- important art inside trim/gutter safety;
- genuine print-ready pixel dimensions;
- consistent Leia face, ears, markings, proportions, accessories, and personality.

## Naming convention

Use sortable names when assets are committed:

`scene-XX_short-description_status.ext`

Examples:
- `scene-04_magic-garden_candidate.png`
- `scene-04_magic-garden_keeper.tif`
- `leia-character-sheet_v01.png`
