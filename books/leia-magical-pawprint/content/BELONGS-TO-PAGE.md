## Composition

- Portrait page at the locked 8.5 × 11 inch trim, no bleed.
- Keep the heading large, friendly, and centered in the upper third.
- Give the name line at least 5.5 inches of clear horizontal writing space and enough vertical breathing room for a young child to write with a crayon or marker.
- Reserve the lower third for one simple Leia vignette after the A02 character model is approved.
- Leia should be secondary to the ownership field: one seated or gently waving pose, crown visible, no complex scenery.
- Optional supporting motifs are limited to 2–4 large pawprints or sparkles. Do not crowd the writing area.
- Maintain the conservative safe-zone rules in `../production/PRINT-ART-SPEC.md`.
- Black line art on white only; no accidental gray fills, textures, or background shading.
